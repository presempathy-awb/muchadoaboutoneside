# Community conduct

Help make this project welcoming to artists, makers and contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Be respectful, discuss work and ideas in good faith, and give clear credit for others' contributions.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. Respect requests to stop contact. Keep criticism about the work and avoid personal attacks.

This policy applies to project-maintained discussions, issues, pull requests and collaboration spaces. Maintainers may remove harmful content, restrict participation, or close discussions to protect participants. They should consider context, use proportionate responses, and avoid publishing private report details.

## Reporting

A dedicated private conduct-reporting channel has not yet been established. Contact a maintainer through an existing project channel to arrange a private report. A public request for a reporting route should omit personal information and incident details. Do not send a report through a person implicated in it if another maintainer is available.

Maintainers should acknowledge reports when possible, limit access to people needed to handle them, explain relevant decisions to affected participants when appropriate, and avoid promising confidentiality or response times they cannot guarantee.

This is the project's own conduct policy. It does not incorporate an external conduct-policy template or license.
