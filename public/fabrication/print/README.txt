  Upright on the integrated oval base
  Organic supports from the build plate for the jaw, chin, and overhangs

Inspect the complete sliced preview in PrusaSlicer before printing. Support
placement, filament, first-layer adhesion, and printer condition must be checked
at denhac. G-code is intentionally not included because it must be generated for
the specific printer and filament loaded at print time.

The maquette is one watertight, positive-volume solid. It was rebuilt from the
stored body, head, and jaw section perimeters, then boolean-unioned with its
base to remove overlaps and self-intersections. It measures approximately
103.53 x 45.90 x 180.00 mm and includes a minimum modeled diameter of 2.4 mm.

The printed plastic is the smooth substrate for the 180 mm foil edition.
Calligraphy is applied on marked aluminum foil after printing, rather than
embossed through the FDM nozzle. Use the matching small-scale kit:
https://muchadoaboutoneside.com/fabrication/small-foil/foil-kit-180mm.zip
Print the paper pattern at 100 percent and test its fit on this exact model.
The 2.4 mm minimum modeled diameter needs a bend test on the chosen foil stock;
successful marking and physical fit have not yet been established.

This is a section-derived printable interpretation. The archival source STL is
not being represented as print-ready.
