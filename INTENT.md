# Project intent

Much Ado About One Side is a sculpture and lettering studio: a place to explore iani's figure-eight snake, read Andrew's poem, prepare workshop references, and share a considered making and exhibition plan.

The **3D design is by [iani schrodinger](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100009324056794)**. Claude and ChatGPT helped with the poem and website. The [README](README.md) records the public site, source links, reuse terms, and provenance.

## What the studio serves today

Visitors can explore the preserved geometry, compare dimensions, download original assets and marking studies, follow making and packing guidance, and edit browser-local poem drafts without an account. A six-foot indoor construction study compares material systems. A projection study develops Enceladus surface and interior imagery, original ritual chimeras, and future score-driven calligraphy cues.

Digital geometry verification supports these studies. It does not prove physical fit, structural adequacy, material acceptance, projector coverage, or burn-event approval. Installation measurements and representative physical samples determine the next fabrication decisions. Mailing arrangements are confirmed directly; the site provides blank handoff fields.

## Principles

- Keep source assets and provenance intact, and distinguish originals from generated studies.
- Preserve the exact canonical poem in marking masters; identify editable drafts and alternate wording clearly.
- Make dimensions, units, downloads, and workshop limitations understandable on small screens and in print.
- Keep anonymous use available. Enable shared editing deliberately and describe who can edit.
- Exclude private references, credentials, and private archive history from public source and releases.
- Welcome adaptations under MIT OR Apache-2.0 while preserving third-party notices.

## Longer-term direction

The [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) proposes voluntary accounts, separate projects and organizations, explicit sharing, versioned uploads, and inventory/procurement integration. Those capabilities require independent access-control, recovery, privacy, and public-build evidence before release. They are not part of the current application's contract.
